VFD Speed Control — PLC + Push-button + HMI

Press FWD / REV / STOP on the operator panel or the HMI, change speed with ▲▼, and watch each step.

① Ready

Line contactor KM1 OFF, DC bus empty. PLC waits for FWD or REV.

② Ramp up

PLC closes KM1, then gives the run signal. VFD raises frequency and voltage together (V/f), 10 Hz per second: soft start, low current.

③ Run at set speed

Speed follows the set point (▲▼ or HMI +/−). In AUTO the PLC PID holds the pump pressure at 4 bar.

④ Ramp down / fault

STOP: VFD ramps to 0 Hz, then KM1 opens. REV: VFD ramps through 0 Hz and turns the other way. Fault: output stops, RESET needed.

🏭 Real example: Booster pump, apartment building

Pump motor 5.5 kW, 400 V, In ≈ 11 A. The VFD slows the pump when demand is low, so it saves energy and avoids water hammer.

  • VFD 7.5 kW (one size up), MCCB 16 A, line contactor 12 A
  • Accel / decel 5 s, min 15 Hz, max 50 Hz, linear V/f
  • Pressure sensor 4–20 mA (0–10 bar) → PLC AI0.0, PID set point 4 bar
  • Pump speed ∝ √(head): at 40 Hz the power is only about half of 50 Hz
  • Wrong rotation gives almost no pressure: press REV and see
